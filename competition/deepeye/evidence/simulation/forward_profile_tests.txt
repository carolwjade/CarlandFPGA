== observed limitations / explicitly NOT run ==
- Software checkpoint only: GREEN here proves deterministic kernel, temporal and serializer behaviour in CPython, nothing about physical
  detection performance. No product card PASS; G03/G09 remain not_run.
- The kernel is a candidate simplified empirical field model, not a Maxwell/full-wave solver and not a calibrated instrument. Formal
  omitted-field/noise bound, independent 3D FDEM applicability check and mesh-convergence gate are NOT RUN. Residual targets in
  simulation_contract_final.json (mesh 0.25 sigma, surrogate rms 0.5, p95 1.0) are untested.
- Prototype cropping outside r<=8 m and outside every 30-degree beam is a numerical rule, not evidence of zero physical response.
- The 8 m support, 30 deg cones, +/-20 deg lobes, 20 Hz update, 100 ms tau, 0.03 log10 white noise, 0.002/sqrt(s) random walk,
  0.2..2000 ohm m envelope, +/-2 differential limit and 0.25 m quadrature step are project assumptions, not vendor data. The
  Halliburton 50 ft / SLB 100 ft figures are commercial marketing maxima and are NOT this model's range.
- Conductivity callback is authority-local truth-side input; no obstacle labels, ids, seeds, screens, editor history or collision
  oracle exist in this module or in the outward record. Air-ranging and opaque conducting-shield contexts are reported invalid
  (source_fault) instead of fabricated readings; magnetic casing, shielding, anisotropy and full-wave effects are not modelled.
- Vibration uses a simplified project AR(1)/ENBW model (a=1/3 at 100 Hz, driving sigma 112.5 ug*sqrt((1+a)/(1-a))); it is not an
  ADXL355 register configuration and carries no measured DAC/XADC gain, offset, quantization or front-end noise evidence. Counts are
  a normalized proxy only.
- Wall-clock authenticity is out of scope: tick monotonicity, 50/10 ms grid alignment and no-backfill ordering are enforced in-module,
  but the authority must drive ticks from the real 100 Hz schedule.
- Host integration gap (reported, not patched): docs/host_contract.json currently whitelists only board telem/event observations and
  has no raw authority-to-bridge sensor sample kind, so this record has no host-schema counterpart yet.

  read sequence; importlib sys.modules registration). The RED failure itself is the module-missing import error, which is unaffected.
